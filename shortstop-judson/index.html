Before you call.

Who do I rent from?

The owner. The gas station and its kitchen are privately owned, and you agree on the schedule, rate and terms directly with them.

Is the equipment included?

Yes. You rent the kitchen with its installed equipment. Ask at your walkthrough which smallwares (pans, utensils, containers) come with it, and bring anything else your menu needs.

What do I need to rent it?

Plan on your own food business permit from the local health authority and liability insurance. Ask the owner what this location needs from you before you sign.

Can I see it first?

Yes. Every rental starts with a walkthrough, so you can check the equipment and the space before you commit.

Can I sell my food in the store?

That's up to the owner. Bring it up at your walkthrough, especially if you're interested in a long-term lease.

Where is it?

Inside the Short Stop Judson gas station. The address is shared when you book a walkthrough.
