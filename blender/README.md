# ShortStop station – Blender model

`shortstop_station.blend` (Blender 5.2) – full 3D model of the Texaco ShortStop, I-35 / SH 46, New Braunfels.
Everything new lives in the collection **20 FINAL station build**:

| Collection | Contents |
|---|---|
| 20a Ground, paving & landscape | concrete paving, raised curbed lawns, red stamped-concrete islands, walks, stall striping (motel, NW lot, storefront incl. ADA) |
| 20b Store | 16.0 x 19.8 m store, glazed storefront, red band, rooftop units, ice chest, propane cage, air machine |
| 20c Canopy & pumps | 32.4 x 12.8 m canopy (lidar height), 4 transverse islands, 8 dispensers |
| 20d Car wash & dumpster | car wash, second outbuilding, dumpster enclosure |
| 20e Signs, flag & spotlight | SE 19.6 m and NE 12.2 m price pylons, 20.3 m flagpole, animated 15 x 28.5 ft US flag, roof spotlight aimed at the flag |
| 20f Roads, signals & street lights | lane markings, crosswalks, mast-arm signals, street lights, I-35 bridges |
| 20g Site lighting, furniture & trees | area lights, live oaks |
| 20h Vehicles | BMW E46 + Cybertruck instances (from `assets/cars`), clearcoat paint per car |
| 20i Context | Edelweiss Inn with teal hip roof |

The brand-art panels on the canopy/pylons use a plain placeholder material – apply the official artwork there.

## Sources
- Building/canopy sizes and heights, flagpole, pylons, street lights: USGS 3DEP 2017 lidar (public domain).
- Roads and neighbouring buildings: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- Lawn, paving, island and stall layout: traced from the owner's Google Earth screenshots (imagery 2023-01-22, (c) Google; the imagery itself is not included).
